VPS questions

Do I get a dedicated IP address?

Yes. Every plan includes one dedicated IPv4 and a /64 IPv6 block, assigned at provisioning and not shared with other customers.

Can I install my own operating system?

Yes. Choose Debian, Ubuntu, AlmaLinux or Rocky Linux when you order, and reinstall from the panel whenever you want.

What happens if I exceed my traffic?

We do not cut you off mid-month. The panel warns you as you approach the limit, and you can add extra traffic or upgrade before it becomes a problem.

Is this a managed service?

The hardware and network are managed by us; the operating system is yours. We will help with anything on the machine, but we do not maintain your applications for you.
