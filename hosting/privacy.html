Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This is a draft. It was written from a generic hosting template and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Confirm your legal entity, actual sub-processors and retention periods with counsel, and make sure every contact address below is monitored before you rely on it.

This policy explains what personal data XyleHosting collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what you can ask us to do with it. It covers this website, the billing area and the server panel.

Who we are

XyleHosting provides game, VPS and dedicated hosting. For personal data about you as a customer, we are the controller: we decide why it is collected and how it is used. For the content you upload to your servers, you are the controller and we act as a processor on your instructions.

Privacy questions: privacy@xyle.host.

What we collect

Account and billing data

  • Name, email address and, where you provide it, a company name or billing address.
  • Payment details. Card numbers are handled by our payment processor; we store only a token and the last four digits.
  • Order history, invoices, plan changes and support correspondence.

Service data

  • Server names, configuration, and the resources your services use.
  • Technical logs needed to operate and secure the platform: IP addresses, timestamps, authentication events, console commands and bandwidth usage.
  • Support tickets you submit.

Website data

  • If you use the server panel, the IP address and browser details in our access logs.
  • Your theme preference, stored locally in your browser so the site remembers whether you chose light or dark. This never leaves your device.

What we do not collect

We do not sell personal data. We do not collect special-category data. We do not build advertising profiles.

Why we collect it, and on what basis

  • To provide the Services. Running your server, allocating resources and supporting you requires account, billing and service data. This is the performance of our contract with you.
  • To take payment and meet tax obligations. Required by law.
  • To keep the platform secure and available. Logs and IP addresses let us investigate abuse and attacks. Our legitimate interest in protecting customers and our infrastructure.
  • To improve the Services. Aggregated, non-identifying usage figures tell us which features people use. Our legitimate interest in improving the product.
  • To send service messages. Order receipts, outage notices and security alerts. Where marketing email is separate, you can opt out at any time.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. The server panel uses strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to protect against cross-site request forgery. Blocking them will prevent you from logging in to the panel. Your light or dark theme choice is kept in local storage, not in a cookie.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the following categories of recipient:

  • Payment processors, to take payment. They act as independent controllers for that part of the data.
  • Infrastructure and support providers that host our systems and answer your tickets, under contract and only as needed to serve you.
  • Professional advisers and authorities, where we are legally required to disclose.
  • A buyer or successor in a merger or sale of assets, with notice to you before your data transfers.

We do not sell personal data to anyone, and we do not share it for anyone else's independent marketing.

Where your data is stored

Servers are located in Nepal, India, Singapore and Germany. If your data is transferred outside your country, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Contact us for a copy of the safeguards we use.

How long we keep it

  • Account and billing records — kept for as long as the account is open, then for the period required by tax and accounting law.
  • Security and access logs — kept for up to 12 months, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Server content — kept until you delete it or the service is terminated, then removed within 30 days.
  • Backups — retained according to the schedule on your plan, and overwritten as new snapshots are taken.
  • Support correspondence — kept for 24 months so we can see the history of an issue.

How we protect it

We encrypt traffic in transit with TLS, restrict access to personal data by role, and log administrative access to production systems. Credentials for servers are issued per server and can be rotated from the panel. No system is perfectly secure, and if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the relevant regulator without undue delay.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, receive a portable copy, and object to automated decision-making.

You can exercise most of these yourself: export or delete server content from the panel, and update your account details in the billing area. For anything else, email privacy@xyle.host. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our services are not directed at children and accounts are limited to adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us data, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change. For a material change we will notify account holders by email at least 30 days beforehand. Previous versions are available on request.

Contact

Write to privacy@xyle.host, or open a ticket in the billing area. Include enough detail for us to find your account.
