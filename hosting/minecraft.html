Minecraft questions

Is Java or Bedrock included?

Both. The panel installs either edition from the same order, so you can switch without moving to a different product.

Can I use my own modpack?

Yes. Upload a server jar over SFTP and set it as the startup command. You keep full file access, so nothing is limited to the plugins we happen to list.

What happens if I go over the player limit?

We do not disconnect players mid-session. You get a warning in the panel and a chance to upgrade, and the server keeps running in the meantime.

Can you move my existing server here?

Yes, free of charge. Open a ticket with your current host details and we will move the world files and keep your address.
