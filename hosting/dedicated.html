Dedicated questions

What does managed support include?

We keep the operating system patched, monitor the hardware, and handle incidents. Running and tuning your game server stays with you unless you ask us otherwise.

Can I bring my own licence keys?

Yes. You supply the keys for Windows and any commercial software. We do not resell licences.

How quickly is hardware replaced?

Failed drives are replaced on the same day where spares are on site. Machines with IPMI can be diagnosed and rebooted remotely without anyone attending the rack.

Is there a contract?

No. These are month-to-month and you can cancel at the end of any billing period.
